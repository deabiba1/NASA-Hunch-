NASA HUNCH Design Challenge 2026

In-Suit Lunar Energy Food Bites Dispenser

A hands-free, zero-crumb dietary delivery mechanism engineered for astronaut safety inside next-generation xEMU spacesuits during extended surface EVAs.

1. The Design Challenge

During 6- to 8-hour Extravehicular Activities (EVAs) on the Moon, crewmembers require continuous nutrition without compromising helmet safety or visibility[cite: 5]. Operating inside a sealed helmet presents severe operational constraints[cite: 5, 6]:

Hands-Free Mouth Access

Astronauts cannot use their hands inside the suit to reach their face[cite: 5]. All extraction and positioning mechanics must be driven entirely by mouth, lip, or chin engagement[cite: 5].

Zero-Crumb Contamination

Loose crumbs in microgravity or reduced gravity float into airflow paths, potentially fouling suit electronics, clouding visors, or causing airway inhalation hazards[cite: 5].

xEMU Helmet Clearance

The dispenser must fit within the strict internal spatial envelope of the Hard Upper Torso (HUT) without impeding field of view or blocking primary anti-fog vent paths[cite: 5, 6].

2. Engineering Process

Step 1: Environmental & Constraints Research

Analyzed microgravity and $\frac{1}{6}g$ lunar environment dynamics[cite: 5]. Identified risks regarding off-gassing, fluid shifts, and mechanical spring detent requirements[cite: 5].

Step 2: CAD Envelope & Mock-up Fitting

Imported official NASA `HUT - Largest.stl` 3D suit geometries to verify clearance zones near the lower collar and cheek zones[cite: 5, 6].

Step 3: Mechanism Prototyping

Engineered positive-indexing spring channels to prevent inadvertent pellet deployment while allowing intuitive bite-and-pull extraction[cite: 5].

Step 4: Encapsulation Testing

Evaluated edible coatings (rice paper, starch films, and gel casings) to contain energy pellet particles without altering taste or structural stability[cite: 5].

3. Key Technical Innovations

Controlled Detent Feed

A precision-tensioned mechanical spring loads 5 to 8 individual food pellets forward without relying on gravity, preventing pellets from "shooting" into the helmet[cite: 5].

Edible Film Encapsulation

Each food pellet is encapsulated in an edible barrier, preventing crumbling and sticky moisture transfer prior to consumption[cite: 5].

Quick-Release Collar Mount

A low-profile mounting latch secures the assembly to the lower HUT ring[cite: 5, 6]. The unit can be attached after suit ingress to prevent snagging during donning[cite: 5].
